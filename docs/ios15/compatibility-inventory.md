## Target coverage and audit notes

- `ios/project.yml` defines `options.deploymentTarget.iOS` as 15.0, inherited by all three targets (`TwinDesk`, `Share`, `Broadcast`). The audited iOS 16 dependencies are in TwinDesk; `Share` and `Broadcast` do not use the listed APIs. The shared app target contains all files under `TwinDesk` (except `WebIOS/**`); `Share` and `Broadcast` compile their own source directories plus `I18n.swift`.
- Other searched APIs in the iOS source are available on iOS 15 or earlier: `safeAreaInset`, `refreshable`, `searchable`, `foregroundStyle`, `textInputAutocapitalization`, `autocorrectionDisabled`, `confirmationDialog`, `fileImporter`, `textSelection`, and the existing one-argument `onChange` usages. They do not require replacement for this deployment change.
- Newer APIs already have runtime fallbacks and are not iOS 15 blockers: `WKWebView.isInspectable` is guarded at iOS 16.4 in `Core/Web.swift`; orientation geometry updates are guarded at iOS 16 in `UI/Pages.swift`; `CLBackgroundActivitySession` is guarded at iOS 17 in `Core/Background.swift`; and iOS 26-only tab bar/background styling is availability-guarded in `UI/RootView.swift`. Keep those guards when changing the target.
- The iOS 16 wording in Settings (`Requires Always on iOS 16`) and the iOS 16 device-name comment in `Core/Store.swift` describe OS behavior, not unguarded API use.

## Availability sources

- Apple's [navigation migration guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/migrating-to-new-navigation-types) explicitly identifies iOS 16 as the minimum for the new navigation types and describes availability-conditional support for earlier OS versions.
- Apple documents [`NavigationStack`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/navigationstack) and [`scrollContentBackground(_:)`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/scrollcontentbackground%28_%3A%29) as SwiftUI APIs; their deployment availability is iOS 16. The related `NavigationSplitView`, typed `navigationDestination`, `presentationDetents`, `scrollDisabled`, and `Layout` APIs are part of the same iOS 16 SwiftUI additions.
- Apple's [SwiftUI Photos picker sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photokit/bringing-photos-picker-to-your-swiftui-app) states that its `PhotosPicker` and `Transferable` sample requires iOS 16; see also the [`PhotosPicker`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photosui/photospicker) and [Core Transferable](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coretransferable) references. `PHPickerViewController` is the earlier UIKit-backed PhotosUI picker (iOS 14+).
- Apple's [iOS 16 release notes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/ios-ipados-release-notes/ios-16-release-notes/) list `scrollContentBackground` among the SwiftUI additions; the [`presentationDetents(_:)`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/presentationdetents%28_%3A%29) reference and [`Layout`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/layout) reference cover the other APIs in this inventory.
- Apple's [App Intents overview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents) describes the system integration used by the Shortcuts provider in `Core/Intents.swift`; App Intents is an iOS 16 framework.
